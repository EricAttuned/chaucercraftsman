About this space

Wake up in our sunny & spacious home in the famous Poets Corner, with all the amenities you need to feel at home! Just a few blocks from gourmet coffee and delicious Mexican, Persian & Spanish food, and a 15-minute walk from the 4th Street shopping district. Just 2 miles from UC Berkeley! Comfortably sleeps 4+ with 2.5 bedrooms and 2.5 baths, and ample parking on a quiet one-way street. Fully stocked kitchen including a gas range, plus a shared patio and yard.

The space

2 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms upstairs. The main bedroom has a king-size bed and ensuite bath. The other bedroom has two twin (or one king) beds and a separate crib for babies and toddlers.

Downstairs features an open flow with a living room and kitchen/dining room. There's a half bath, a fully stocked kitchen, and a spare bonus room which can be used as a third bedroom.

Other things to note

This property is on a lot with two homes. This home is in the front, and there is a home in the back occupied by tenants. The yard is shared, so we ask that guests be mindful of noise.
